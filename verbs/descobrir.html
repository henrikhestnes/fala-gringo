How to use descobrir in Brazilian Portuguese

Descobrir is the Brazilian Portuguese verb for to find out, to discover. It is an irregular verb: 1 of its forms in the three tenses below break the regular -ir pattern (descubro), and those are the ones to learn by heart.

Three tenses do almost all the work in conversation. The presente covers the present and the near future: eu descubro — I find out. The pretérito perfeito is the finished past, the English simple past: eu descobri — I found out. The pretérito imperfeito is for habits, background and how things used to be: eu descobria — I used to find out.

Said the Rio way, descubro sounds like desh-KOO-broo. The nós form descobrimos ends in the carioca “sh”, desh-koh-BREE-moosh — the most recognisable sound of the Rio accent, where São Paulo says a plain s.
