How to use comprar in Brazilian Portuguese

Comprar is the Brazilian Portuguese verb for to buy. It is a regular -ar verb, so once you know the endings there are no surprises.

Three tenses do almost all the work in conversation. The presente covers the present and the near future: eu compro — I buy. The pretérito perfeito is the finished past, the English simple past: eu comprei — I bought. The pretérito imperfeito is for habits, background and how things used to be: eu comprava — I used to buy / was buying. Because comprar is one of the 40 most-used verbs, the page also has the imperfeito do subjuntivo, the tense of hypotheticals and wishes: eu comprasse — if I bought.

Said the Rio way, compro sounds like KOHM-proo. The nós form compramos ends in the carioca “sh”, kohm-PRAH-moosh — the most recognisable sound of the Rio accent, where São Paulo says a plain s.
