How to use sonhar in Brazilian Portuguese

Sonhar is the Brazilian Portuguese verb for to dream. It is a regular -ar verb, so once you know the endings there are no surprises.

Three tenses do almost all the work in conversation. The presente covers the present and the near future: eu sonho — I dream. The pretérito perfeito is the finished past, the English simple past: eu sonhei — I dreamed. The pretérito imperfeito is for habits, background and how things used to be: eu sonhava — I used to dream / was dreaming.

Said the Rio way, sonho sounds like SOH-nyoo. The nós form sonhamos ends in the carioca “sh”, soh-NYAH-moosh — the most recognisable sound of the Rio accent, where São Paulo says a plain s.
