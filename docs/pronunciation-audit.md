# Pronunciation audit — September 2026

Scope: all 1,984 stored verb rows (including 232 subjunctive rows), with a cross-tense comparison of identical spellings and a targeted review of generated hint conventions. The engine still displays hand-maintained forms; no runtime pronunciation generator was added.

Found 20 forms with inconsistent hints across tenses. Standardized their syllable boundaries, nasal hints and hard-g spelling. Also corrected related generated hints: Rio coda s before k/p/t uses **sh**, and seguir keeps hard **gh** before i. In descemos/desci, **sc** represents one s sound, not two syllables. `ny` represents nh; the whole stressed syllable is capitalized.

The new data check rejects inconsistent hints for identical verb forms. The generated verb pages are rebuilt from the corrected source.

These are English-based learning aids, not IPA transcriptions. Nasal vowels, vowel quality and Rio r cannot be represented precisely by English spelling. Audio is restricted to a matching pt-BR voice, but a browser voice is not a guarantee of a specifically carioca accent. A native-speaker recording review remains a separate form of validation; this audit does not claim one occurred.

## Follow-up — review pass (branch review/app-improvements)

A second sweep, driven by the review notes, made the remaining hints follow the same rules and extended them to the non-verb topics:

- **-eu perfeito** is the [ew] diphthong, hinted `EH-oo` like comeu / entendeu / vendeu — never `AY-oo`, which is the `-ei` sound. A data check now rejects any form ending in `-eu` whose hint does not end in `EH-oo` (deveu, esqueceu, dependeu, resolveu, respondeu were the offenders).
- **Coda s** is `sh` everywhere, not only before k/p/t: the rows still starting `es-` (estar, esquecer, estudar, escrever), gosto / mostro / assisto, and the word-final s of fiz `FEESH`, quis `KEESH`, pus `POOSH`.
- **di / de → dʒi** is `djee` (depende, responde, respondia…), as their siblings already had.
- **pode vs pôde** no longer share a hint: presente `PAW-djee` (open o), perfeito `POH-djee`.
- **l before a consonant or word-final** is the Brazilian [w], written `ow` / `w` as in legal `leh-GOW` and azul `ah-ZOOW`: almoçar `ow-moh-SAH-…`, voltar `VOW-too`, resolver `heh-ZOW-voo`, abril `ah-BREEW`, "Se acalma!" `ah-KOW-mah`.
- **nh** stays inside one syllable as `ny`: tenho `TEH-nyoo`, venho, ponho, ganho, cozinho, junho `ZHOO-nyoo`, azul-marinho `mah-REE-nyoo`.
- **dez** is voiceless: `DEHSH`.
- **Non-verb topics** (nouns, adjectives, adverbs, numbers, pronominal) now use the verbs' system — final `-ss` / `-s` → `-sh` (dois `DOYSH`, depois `deh-POYSH`), s before k/p/t → `sh` (escola `eesh-KOH-lah`, resposta `hesh-POHSH-tah`), `jee` → `djee` (cidade `see-DAH-djee`) — and every glossary expression gained a hint in the same system (`pronHint`, e.g. pode ser `PAW-djee SEHR`).
